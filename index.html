Word Trace

New England Academy Paleontology

Drag through touching tiles, diagonals included, to spell a word from this chapter’s vocabulary. Each tile can be used once per word. Phrases are found one word at a time, in any order: Plate tectonics is PLATE and TECTONICS, traced separately. If a word belongs to two terms, trace it twice.

You can also tap tiles one at a time and tap the last one again to check, or type and press Enter. Clue gives a hint for a term but halves its points. Helpers unlock as you go: Start counts at 30% of the words, Letter counts and Fade finished at 50%, and the Word bank at 65%. Finishing a grid earns a bonus, and the bonus is biggest if you never turn on a helper.
